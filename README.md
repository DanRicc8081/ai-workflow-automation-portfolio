## 🚀 Projects

### 1. Telegram Lead Qualification Bot

AI-powered lead qualification workflow that processes Telegram messages, classifies lead intent, logs leads into Google Sheets, and triggers automated follow-up workflows.

**Business use case:**  
Helps small businesses respond faster to inbound Telegram leads and avoid losing potential customers.

**Features**

- AI intent classification
- Lead routing
- Telegram auto-replies
- CRM logging in Google Sheets
- Fallback handling
- Notification workflows

**Tools:** OpenAI, Make, Google Sheets, Telegram Bot API, Webhooks

### 2. AI Support Ticket Routing System

AI-assisted support workflow that classifies incoming support tickets by category, urgency, and priority score, then routes them to the correct support path.

**Business use case:**  
Helps support teams triage requests faster, prioritize urgent issues, and keep ticket information structured.

**Features**

- AI ticket classification
- Urgency and priority scoring
- Structured JSON output
- Routing by ticket category
- Google Sheets ticket logging
- Optional email confirmation

**Tools:** OpenAI, Make, Tally, Google Sheets, Gmail, JSON parsing

AI-assisted email workflow that analyzes incoming emails, classifies the message type, generates a draft response, and keeps a human review step before sending.

**Features**

- Email classification
- AI-generated draft replies
- Human review before sending
- Gmail workflow integration
